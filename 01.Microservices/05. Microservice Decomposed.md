When migrating or building an e-commerce platform, breaking down a monolithic architecture into microservices requires grouping related business capabilities into distinct functional domains.


---

## 📦 1. Main Microservices
Directly customer-facing services managing core application domain entities:
* **Users:** Handles administrative user profiles and credentials.
* **Customers:** Manages customer account details, preferences, and saved addresses.
* **Discount:** Manages coupon validation, promo codes, and pricing deals.
* **Product:** Manages product catalogs, categories, variants, and stock displays.
* **Shopping Cart:** Handles active user session baskets and real-time cart updates.
* **Orders:** Serves as the primary entry point for order creation and order status tracking.

---

## 💳 2. Order Transactional Microservices
Backend event-driven services handling financial operations and fulfillment workflows:
* **Orders:** Manages complex transactional order state machines across the fulfillment lifecycle.
* **Inventory:** Handles stock count reservations, warehouse holds, and item deductions.
* **Billing:** Generates invoices, receipts, and accounting statements.
* **Payment:** Integrates with payment gateways (Stripe, PayPal) to authorize and capture charges.
* **Shipping:** Manages third-party logistics dispatch, shipping labels, and tracking status.
* **Notification:** Asynchronously delivers confirmation emails, SMS, and push notifications.

---

## 🧠 3. Intelligence Microservices
Data-driven analytical services providing security, personalization, and localized logic:
* **Identity:** Handles Single Sign-On (SSO), JWT tokens, and role-based authorization.
* **Marketing:** Executes targeted campaigns, promotional newsletters, and customer segmentation.
* **Location:** Calculates local store stock, geofencing, and location-based taxes/currencies.
* **Rating:** Manages product reviews, ratings, and customer feedback.
* **Recommendation:** Uses machine learning models to suggest related items based on browsing history.

---

## 📊 Summary Comparison

| Domain Category | Primary Focus | Communication Style | Scalability Driver |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Main Microservices** | Core Entities & Catalog Browsing | Synchronous REST / gRPC | High Read Traffic |
| **Order Transactional** | Checkout Execution & Fulfillment | Asynchronous Messaging (Kafka/NATS) | Data Integrity & Throughput |
| **Intelligence** | Analytics, Auth & Personalization | Async Events & Internal APIs | Data Compute & ML Workloads |
